Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TechHub Profile Sync Chrome Extension ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our extension.

1. Single Purpose Statement

The sole purpose of the TechHub Profile Sync extension is to seamlessly synchronize your public profile data and post statistics from the TechHub platform to a designated Supabase database, and to assist you by automating repetitive interactions (such as liking and commenting) specifically within the TechHub ecosystem.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with our synchronization and automation features, we collect the following information:

  • Profile Information: We access your public TechHub profile data (such as username, full name, email, avatar, department, and oxygen score) directly from the TechHub website.
  • Authentication Data: We securely intercept and temporarily store necessary authentication tokens (Cookies and CSRF tokens) generated by your active TechHub session.
  • Interaction Data: We monitor and record your extension-driven interactions (likes, comments, and posts) for statistical and synchronization purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the functionality of the extension:

  • To synchronize your TechHub profile and post statistics with the connected Supabase database.
  • To securely authenticate and authorize background automated requests (such as auto-liking and auto-commenting) on your behalf on the TechHub platform.
  • To display your personal statistics and interaction history within the extension's user interface.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are stored securely in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and are never transmitted to any third-party services other than the designated TechHub API endpoints.

Your profile data and synchronization statistics are transmitted securely to a dedicated Supabase database explicitly configured for this extension. We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access or alteration of your data.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your data with any third parties, advertisers, or external tracking services. Your data is strictly exchanged between your local browser, the TechHub platform, and the configured Supabase database.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can stop the collection and synchronization of data at any time by uninstalling the extension or disconnecting your account within the extension interface.
  • Uninstalling the extension will automatically clear the locally stored authentication data and preferences from your browser.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact the developer via the official support channels or the Chrome Web Store support tab.
